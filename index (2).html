Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Unmotivated Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Chill Frogs mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information. However, our App uses third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google AdMob — displays ads and may collect the Advertising ID to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash reports to help us improve stability. No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Google Play Billing — handles in-app purchases. Payment information is processed entirely by Google.

How We Use Information

  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob
  • To monitor and fix app crashes via Firebase Crashlytics
  • To process in-app purchases via Google Play

Advertising ID

Our App uses the Android Advertising ID to display personalised ads through Google AdMob. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device's Google Settings under Ads.

Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Retention

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device only. We do not store personal data on our servers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at unmotivatedstudios2026@gmail.com.
